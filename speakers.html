Keynote speaker / 01

Professor Nicole Hondow

Professor of Materials Characterisation
University of Leeds

Nicole Hondow studies how advanced materials behave in complex environments. Her work uses analytical electron microscopy to investigate nanomaterials relevant to medicine, catalysis, energy and the environment. She was promoted to Professor at Leeds in April 2026.

Research expertise

Materials characterisation · Electron microscopy · Nanoparticles

Keynote speaker / 03

Dr hab. Arkadiusz Przybysz, Prof. SGGW

Centre for Climate Research
Warsaw University of Life Sciences – SGGW

Arkadiusz Przybysz researches the relationships between plants and environmental quality. Based at the SGGW Centre for Climate Research, his expertise includes air pollution, phytoremediation, ecosystem services, urban greenery and plant physiology.

Research expertise

Phytoremediation · Air pollution · Urban greenery

Keynote speaker / 04

Associate Professor Dr Dzun Noraini Jimat

Kulliyyah of Engineering
International Islamic University Malaysia

Dzun Noraini Jimat is an Associate Professor at IIUM’s Kulliyyah of Engineering. Her research interests include microbial fermentation, cell immobilisation, nanofibre synthesis and characterisation, and bioprocess design and optimisation. She holds a PhD from Newcastle University.

Research expertise

Microbial fermentation · Nanofibres · Bioprocess optimisation

Keynote speaker / 05

Associate Professor Dr Elmi bin Abu Bakar

School of Aerospace Engineering
Universiti Sains Malaysia

Elmi bin Abu Bakar is an Associate Professor at USM’s School of Aerospace Engineering. His expertise spans machine vision, instrumentation and control, robotics and manufacturing inspection. His research includes non-destructive evaluation, image-based monitoring and industrial process improvement.

Research expertise

Machine vision · Non-destructive testing · Manufacturing inspection
